avla:- a:v; ‘to hunt (wild game)’; , with some phonetic changes. av avlasar ‘when he hunted wild game’ 9: avlap ölürdi ‘he hunted and killed’ (innumerable wild animals) 34, 57: beğ av avla:dı: ‘the beg hunted wild game’ (ṣāda . . . al-ṣayd) 287 (avla:r, avla:ma:k): işṭāda ‘to hunt’ a:wla:- 22, 11; 103: awla:- (spelt) (1) şikār kardan ‘to hunt’ (and (2) mālīdan, in Arabic dalaka, ‘to rub, polish’; hardly to be regarded as a translation of avla:-; inexplicable) 21: avla- ‘to hunt’ 16; 271: işṭāda mina’l-ṣayd awla:- 34, 4; ṣāda mina’l-ṣayd awla:- (vocalized u:la:-) 41, 11: (awlu: ‘hunting grounds’, the verb derived from it is) awla- işṭāda 25: ṣāda awla- 8: avla- ‘to hunt’ (actually ‘to fish’) 53.

Gerard Clauson, 1972, p. 10.
